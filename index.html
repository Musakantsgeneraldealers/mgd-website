About Us

Musakants General Dealers is a business committed to providing reliable and quality products and services to individuals, institutions and organisations. We specialise in the supply of educational materials, construction materials and household products, as well as providing construction services. Our commitment is to customer satisfaction, quality service delivery and building lasting relationships with our customers.

Company Background

Musakants General Dealers (MGD) is a wholly Zambian owned, managed and registered company, with its operational base in the Northwestern Province, Zambia and it is based in Zambezi District. Musakants General Dealers (MGD) was founded in January 2023 as a Supplier of Hardware and Stationery, Groceries, Solar Panels and installation, Transportation of goods, Human Resource Supply and Fast-Moving Consumer Goods (FMCG). The company is also involved in Construction of Clinics, Schools and Staff Houses.

Mission And Values

Musakants General Dealer's mission is to provide our clients with a reliable and quality service through our qualified and skilled personnel. Our primary goal is to target Government sectors, Mining, Industrial and Commercial clients. Musakants General Dealers (MGD) strives to create work and to uplift individuals in the community through sustainable development projects and it is committed to building capacity in its employees and associated subcontractors. As a company, we also pride ourselves of being able to deliver on all our contractual obligations within the stipulated period to the satisfaction of all our clients. Our past performance records show that we make good of all delivery promises in a very timely manner.

Vision

To provide affordable, quality products and dependable services while maintaining professionalism, integrity and customer satisfaction.
